Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one complete realistic 3D miniature player-city fortress model asset for the WWII-inspired strategy game 山河远征录, matching its existing A / garden citadel architectural collection.

Primary request:
Create an impressive, prestigious, high-end military fortress-city (玩家军事要塞重镇) with deep military atmosphere and impeccable architectural order. The city seamlessly combines a low perimeter defensive bastion wall, a grand neoclassical city hall command center, a prominent air-defense radar station, an authentic prisoner-of-war (POW) camp compound, and an expansive multi-branch military base with both armored staging grounds and an active airfield.

Camera and Framing:

- Near-orthographic focal length, entire fortress fully contained within the square frame with a 6-8 percent clean margin. All-in-focus rendering from front gate to rear radar tower.

Architectural Zones and Mandatory Facilities:
1. Fortified Perimeter Wall (要塞城墙):
   Low, thick early-20th-century stone and reinforced concrete defensive rampart enclosing the city. Corner bastions with mounted dual anti-aircraft flak guns, parapet walkway with embrasures, and a formidable southern armored entrance gatehouse with heavy reinforced gates, guard posts, and anti-vehicle barricades.
2. Grand City Hall (市政厅 / 统帅大本营):
   Commanding the north-central axis as the premier architectural anchor: a magnificent 3-4 story neoclassical civic-military headquarters with a pale limestone facade, grand columned portico, wide stone approach steps, symmetrical wings, and a modest central clock tower with an oxidized bronze dome. A dignified paved command plaza extends before it.
3. Air-Defense Radar Station (防空雷达站):
   Elevated on the northeastern fortified bastion: a tall steel-lattice antenna tower supporting an open wire-mesh parabolic radar dish, accompanied by a slender radio communication mast and a reinforced concrete transmitter bunker.
4. POW Camp Compound (战俘营):
   Distinct, walled-off compound in the northwestern quarter: two long, low single-story barracks with red terracotta tile roofs and pale stucco walls, enclosed by timber posts and taut wire-mesh fencing surrounding a packed-dirt muster yard, guarded by two elevated timber watchtowers at opposite corners with four slender legs and pitched tile roofs.
5. Comprehensive Military Base Complex (综合军事基地):
   - East sector airfield: smooth neutral-gray concrete runway and taxiway, curved corrugated-iron aircraft hangars, small glass-cab flight tower, and organized parked WWII propeller warplanes (P-51 Mustang style fighters and heavy B-17 Flying Fortress style bombers, parked with landing gear firmly on the apron).
   - South sector armored parade & staging grounds: paved assembly yard displaying disciplined rows of olive-drab medium/heavy tanks with clear turrets and barrels, six-wheeled M8 Greyhound style reconnaissance cars, and BM-13 Katyusha rocket launcher trucks with exposed rails.
   - West-central ordnance depot: red-brick armaments workshop with steel sawtooth glass roofs, supply crates, fuel drums, and transport trucks.
